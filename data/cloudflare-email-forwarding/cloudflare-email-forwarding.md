## Overview

Cloudflare Email Forwarding is a free email forwarding service integrated with Cloudflare's DNS and security offerings. It enables users to create custom email addresses on their own domain and forward messages to existing email accounts.

## Features

- Free email forwarding with custom domains
- Spam filtering
- Unlimited domains
- Free SSL
- DNS management included

## Pricing

Free with Cloudflare's free DNS plan.

## Deployment

Cloud (Cloudflare)
