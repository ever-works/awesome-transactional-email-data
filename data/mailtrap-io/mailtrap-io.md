## Overview

Mailtrap.io provides email testing and sending infrastructure.

## Key Features

- Email API and SMTP
- 4,000 emails per month free (limited to 150 emails/day)
- Email Marketing: 500 contacts and 1,500 emails per month
- Email Sandbox: 50 test emails per month, one sandbox, up to 10 stored emails

## Pricing

Free tier available. Paid plans for higher volumes.
