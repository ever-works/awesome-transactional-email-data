## Overview

HCL Domino is a commercial email and collaboration server platform originally developed by IBM as Lotus Domino.

## Features

- SMTP, IMAP, and POP3 email protocols
- Calendaring and scheduling
- Application development platform
- Internet password lockout
- SMTP inbound routing restrictions
- DKIM signing for outbound messages
- Spam reduction configuration options

## Pricing

Commercial product with paid licensing. Subscription plans available.

## Deployment

Available for on-premises and cloud deployment.
