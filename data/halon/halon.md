## Overview

Halon is a commercial SMTP server and email infrastructure platform designed for high-volume email sending.

## Features

- Programmable scripting engine for email processing
- Custom routing and filtering
- DANE support for encrypted SMTP connections
- DKIM signing and verification
- Spam filtering integration
- High-performance email delivery
