## Overview

Zoho ZeptoMail is a transactional email service that combines pay-as-you-go pricing with 24/7 email and phone assistance on all plans, including the free tier. It is part of the Zoho suite of applications.

## Features

- Drag-and-drop email builder with customizable templates
- Multiple mail agents for email segmentation by purpose, domain, or application
- Separate email infrastructure for transactional delivery to preserve sender reputation
- 24/7 email and phone support on all plans
- Pay-as-you-go credit system (credits valid for 6 months)
- Analytics per mail agent
- Zapier integrations for automation

## Pricing

Free for up to 10,000 emails. One credit costs £2 (1 credit = 10,000 emails), valid for 6 months.

## Notes

- Setup may require technical assistance for SMTP or API configuration
- Email analytics described as underwhelming
