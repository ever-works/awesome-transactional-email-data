## Overview

Mailmodo is an email marketing platform that enables creating and sending interactive AMP emails. It is designed for transactional emails that include interactive elements within the email content itself.

## Features

- Interactive AMP email editor with drag-and-drop interface
- Over 300 interactive email templates
- Various widgets for gamification, forms, and product recommendations
- Automatically fetches brand logo, fonts, and colors into emails
- Domain reputation score monitoring
- Deliverability infrastructure for transactional sending
- Zapier integrations for automation

## Pricing

Free trial for 21 days. Paid plans begin at $49/month for 2,500 contacts and 20,000 emails. Pricing is tied to the number of contacts. Mailmodo offers discounted plans for NGOs.

## Limitations

- No free plan available
- Interactive emails require email clients that support AMP
- Requires strong email reputation and domain reputation monitoring
