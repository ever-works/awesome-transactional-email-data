## Overview

DMARCmetric monitors DMARC reports for email deliverability.

## Key Features

- DMARC report monitoring
- Named sending sources
- SPF/DKIM alignment
- Plain-English summaries
- Free for 1 domain: 10,000 messages/month, 30-day history, email alerts
- No credit card required

## Pricing

Free tier (1 domain). Paid plans for additional domains.
