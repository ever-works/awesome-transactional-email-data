## Overview

Cyrus IMAP is an open-source IMAP and POP3 mail server developed by Carnegie Mellon University. It is designed for scalability and efficiency in large-scale email environments.

## License

Released under a BSD-style license.

## Features

- IMAP and POP3 protocols
- Designed for large-scale deployment
- RFC standards compliance
- Scalable mailbox storage
- Efficient message delivery
