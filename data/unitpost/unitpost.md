## Overview

Unitpost is an AI-first transactional and marketing email service designed for developers.

## Key Features

- Free tier: 5,000 emails/month, 200 emails/day
- 10,000 contacts and 5 domains included
- No credit card required for the free plan
- SDKs, MCP, and REST API available
- Supports both transactional and marketing email use cases

## Pricing

Free tier available (5,000 emails/month). Paid plans for higher volumes.
