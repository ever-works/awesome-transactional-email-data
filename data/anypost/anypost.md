## Overview

Anypost is a transactional and broadcast email API service for developers.

## Key Features

- 3,000 emails per month free
- Pricing as low as 8¢ per 1,000 emails after free tier
- Transactional and broadcast email support

## Pricing

Free tier (3,000 emails/month). Paid plans starting at 8¢ per 1,000 emails.
