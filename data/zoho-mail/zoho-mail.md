## Overview

Zoho Mail is an email hosting service that provides business email with custom domain support. It is part of the broader Zoho suite of business applications and offers a forever-free plan for small teams.

## Features

- Free for up to 5 users
- 5 GB storage per user
- 25 MB attachment limit
- One custom domain
- Webmail access
- Mobile apps

## Pricing

Free plan for up to 5 users. Paid plans available for additional users and storage.

## Deployment

Cloud (Zoho-hosted)
